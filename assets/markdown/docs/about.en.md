# About DISC

The DISC application was originally developed by a team of five Bachelor’s Computer Science students at Delft University of Technology (TU Delft). As part of the second-year Software Project course (CSE2000), the team was challenged to design and build a software solution that addressed a real-world problem provided by an external client.

The client for the project was the [TU Delft Multimedia Computing Group](https://www.tudelft.nl/ewi/over-de-faculteit/afdelingen/intelligent-systems/multimedia-computing/research/speechlab), whose researchers were developing personalized speech recognition models for individuals with dysarthria. Their goal was to enable users to benefit from these specialized models on their own devices through an accessible and user-friendly application.

To address this need, the DISC application was created. It allows users to upload and manage one or more personalized speech recognition models, making speech recognition technology more accessible to individuals with speech impairments.
